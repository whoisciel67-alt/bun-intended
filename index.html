How to play

Move around your bakery and visit the stations. Bake a few treats before customers arrive, then serve the orders shown below the game.

  • [ A ] / [ D ] or [ ← ] / [ → ] — walk
  • [ E ] — use the nearby station
  • Esc — close a window
  • Space — serve the next waiting order

This is an early prototype. The bakery is small on purpose. Our lovely dev (Ciel) will add more recipes, characters and story moments as we go.
